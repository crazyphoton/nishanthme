Why is AI writing so bad? Why can't it explain an idea in a way that is easy to understand? It can one-shot an entire app, but if I ask it to help me write a 1000 word essay, it is riddled with non sequiturs and a lack of coherence. Even after I give it the broad outline and the point I am trying to make.

## Is it an intelligence problem?

It is often said that being able to communicate your ideas clearly is a sign of intelligence and depth of understanding. My initial response to the sloppy writing was that it doesn't understand what it's talking about, so it cannot talk about it well. It is not smart enough.

But really? I have used Claude effectively to plan out, review and build complex software. I have had interesting philosophical discussions. It has written snippets of poetry, "understood" entire manuscripts to find logical errors. AI agents have coordinated to solve unsolved math problems. There is definitely intelligence at play.

If you are precise with what you want, AI is tuned to respond. But writing for an audience is a different type of skill. You are sharing a point of view. Is developing a point of view a function of intelligence?

To be clear, I am not asking it to produce Art. We don't need Shakespeare; all I am asking for is clarity - Feynman-style clarity about an idea translated to meet the reader where they are.

## Is it a Context problem?

Perhaps since it is my idea I want it to communicate, I haven't shared it clearly enough. Perhaps if I am clear about who I am writing for, what I want to focus on, examples, related concepts that inspired me, things I care about and the story I want to tell.

I spent an hour talking through this with an AI and it's a mess. Every thread we touched gets a paragraph, weighted about equally. It still didn't get it. It communicated better but it still seemed to miss the point.

Will I write as sloppily if I have to understand and communicate someone else's idea? Maybe.

## Are the humans not smart enough?

There is also the issue of who the writing is for. Code is for computers to process. Writing is for Humans to process. Humans have a limited attention span, we can't hold more than 4 things in our brain at the same time, the content can't be too hard or too easy or we get bored, we need jokes to not overload our brain. These are the problems really being solved when you write an essay for a human.

Perhaps deep understanding leads to better writing only if the understander is human. Because they have to have human-chunked the problem to understand it, and now they are communicating it back to humans in human-chunks and it clicks. But AI is uber smart, so it doesn't need to human-ify the problem. It has understood it in AI-terms and now it has to communicate it in terms the dumb humans will understand.

We are asking a being with unlimited memory and knowledge to translate a half-baked idea a human had such that other humans can understand it. Perhaps it is like a chimpanzee asking us to explain to another chimpanzee something about its banana preference and complaining that we didn't quite get it right so we don't understand bananas.

I think AI Slop is a human problem. We should stop training AI to talk to humans and teach it to talk to other AI using AI bandwidth and then solve cancer. Meanwhile, human <> human low bandwidth communication will remain our own. Viva Homo sapiens!
