Before drafting, I had Claude argue the crux of the post with an imagined skeptical reader, a backend engineer who thinks AI writing is bad because the models aren't good enough yet. The essay was then written from this argument, not from a brief. This is the second run of that dialogue, after I'd told Claude about the research-paper conversation.

**Claude:** You think the writing is bad because the model isn't smart enough.

**Engineer:** It writes buggy prose the way GPT-3 wrote buggy code. Next version fixes it.

**Claude:** Have you had it explain a paper to you lately?

**Engineer:** Yeah, last week. It was fine, actually.

**Claude:** Fine how?

**Engineer:** I asked questions, it answered. By the end I got the paper.

**Claude:** Now ask it to write a blog post about that paper for your team.

**Engineer:** I did. It was unreadable. Every section had the same weight. I had to rewrite it.

**Claude:** Same model, same paper, same week. What changed?

**Engineer:** In the chat I was there. I kept saying "wait, what's a KV cache" and it would back up.

**Claude:** So where did the good explanation come from?

**Engineer:** From me interrupting it, I guess.

**Claude:** And for the blog post, who was interrupting?

**Engineer:** Nobody. It just... wrote.

**Claude:** So which part was the model missing? The paper, or the interruptions?

**Engineer:** The interruptions. It had the paper the whole time.

**Claude:** When you explain your system to a new hire, where does your cut-down version come from?

**Engineer:** I remember what confused me. And what confused the last three hires.

**Claude:** You carry around a record of where people trip. The model doesn't. In the chat you supplied it live, one trip at a time. For the blog post there was nothing to write against, so it wrote for nobody.

**Engineer:** So it's not that it doesn't understand the paper.

**Claude:** It understands the paper fine. It has no idea what a human doesn't understand about it.

**Engineer:** ...oh. That's not a smartness thing. That's a who-tripped-on-what thing.

**Claude:** And the only way to get it is from a human who tripped.
